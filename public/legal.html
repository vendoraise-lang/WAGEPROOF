What the product does

Proposal Preflight applies structured rules to explicit numeric commitments, support coverage, process detail and response coverage. Unrecognized requirements use matching only. It does not use a paid AI API or generate an expert assessment. Results may be incomplete or misleading. You must independently verify compliance, commitments, factual accuracy, quantities, deadlines, formatting and attachments. There is no certification, legal advice or bid-success guarantee.

Your text and privacy

Do not submit credentials, personal information or confidential material you are not authorized to process. Text is transmitted to this service for in-memory analysis and is not intentionally retained on the server. Draft text and a random checkout key are stored in this tab's session storage until you clear them or close the tab, subject to browser session-restoration behavior. Clear the text using the provided button and use a trusted device.

Stripe processes payments and receives a keyed report fingerprint and a hashed browser key, not the proposal or checklist. Our application does not receive card details. Hosting providers may process network metadata and access logs under their own policies. Application diagnostics record aggregate request counts and sanitized errors, not document text. The optional first-party factory stores daily aggregate pageviews, review completions, downloads and error counts for 90 days, without document contents, IP addresses or user identifiers. Aggregate observations are visible on the operations dashboard; they are event counts, not unique people. Test events are segregated. No advertising trackers are included.
