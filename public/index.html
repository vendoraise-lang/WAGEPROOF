What exactly does it check?

It compares explicit duration, uptime, retention, reference and experience thresholds, including unit conversion. It flags conflicting values, missing 24/7 commitments, restated requirements and process claims lacking operational detail. Unrecognized requirements fall back to response-coverage matching. Attachments, SLA definitions, truth and legal eligibility require your review.

What do I receive for $9?

A report for up to 30 requirements against one specific draft, downloadable as HTML and CSV. There is no subscription. This is a self-service preflight review, not professional proposal writing. Full reports are free during the test beta. You can use the free preview before buying.

Can I upload a PDF or Word document?

No. Paste plain text from your document. Formatting, tables, figures and attachments are not checked. Do not paste secrets or sensitive personal data.

How do payments and report recovery work?

Stripe handles card details. Return to this same tab after payment; your text is needed to regenerate the report. Download it before clearing storage. If delivery fails, use the support address on the legal page and provide your Stripe receipt, not your proposal text.
