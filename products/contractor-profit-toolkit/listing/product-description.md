# Contractor Profit Toolkit — Single-Job Estimate & Cost Tracker

Know how your job budget compares with costs incurred and customer payments, in one offline workbook.

For small US trade contractors who want a straightforward job-level planning and tracking sheet. Build cost-based estimates, track approved change orders, record actual costs and payments, and review contribution margins and uncollected contract balances.

Includes a blank XLSX workbook, a clearly fictional worked example and a PDF quick-start/user guide. Blue input cells, protected formulas, category/status dropdowns and incomplete-entry warnings help you use it consistently.

One job per file; reuse a copy for each job. Capacity per file: 100 estimate lines, 200 costs, 100 changes and 100 payments. USD. No macros, accounts, subscriptions or external services.

Important: this is not accounting software, a cash-flow ledger, tax calculation, invoice generator or a completion forecast. Unfinished-job margins are provisional.
