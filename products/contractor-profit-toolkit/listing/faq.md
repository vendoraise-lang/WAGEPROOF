# Customer FAQ

**Do I need a subscription or internet?** No. Download once and use locally with a compatible spreadsheet editor.

**Is Excel included?** No. LibreOffice is a free local option; Microsoft Excel is sold separately. Recalculation tested in LibreOffice, not Excel UI.

**Can I reuse it?** Yes, for your own business's jobs. Save one copy per job. Do not redistribute or resell.

**Does it calculate final profit?** Only your fully reconciled inputs can support a final contribution figure. Unfinished jobs omit future costs; company overhead and taxes may also be excluded.

**Is markup the same as margin?** No: 25% markup on $1,000 creates a $1,250 price and 20% margin.

**Are overhead and contingency added to the quote?** No. Set line markups to cover them. Contingency is displayed separately as a reserve.

**Do pending changes count?** No revenue/budget contribution until Approved. Actual costs count whenever incurred, regardless of approval.

**Can I record refunds or deductive changes?** Yes, negative payments for refunds and negative approved change values for deductions. Explain each adjustment.

**Does it track invoices, taxes, payroll or cash flow?** No. Customer receipts and incurred expenses have different timing; their difference is not cash flow.

**Can I add unlimited rows?** No. Use the stated fixed capacities. Added rows may not be summed.

**Is my project data uploaded?** The workbook has no upload or external connections. Your storage/editor choices determine privacy.

**Will it run on my phone or Google Sheets?** Those interfaces are unverified; desktop local use is recommended.

**What support/refund policy applies?** The seller must publish its real support contact and applicable policy before accepting purchases. No policy is invented here.
