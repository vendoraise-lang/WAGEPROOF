## Payment readiness and concrete blocker
Stripe official https://stripe.com/global was retrieved: Morocco is not listed for standard payment-account availability. Stablecoin treasury/global buyer access does not establish Moroccan payment-account eligibility. Do not use an invented foreign operator or assume direct Stripe eligibility.

Lemon Squeezy is now the recommended provider to apply to and test, conditional on actual operator/store/product approval. Official documentation was successfully retrieved 2026-10-11; the earlier network blocker is resolved:
- https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries explicitly lists Morocco for bank payouts. US buyers are not on its unsupported-customer-country list. This is country-level support, not approval of this operator or bank.
- https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees shows the base 5% + $0.50 on total order value including tax. Additional charges: 1.5% for outside-US transactions, 1.5% for PayPal, 0.5% for subscriptions. Non-US bank payouts cost 1%; non-US PayPal payouts 3% capped at $30. US buyers and Moroccan payouts have distinct fee bases; neither implies a US operator. At $9 before any tax/add-ons, base fee is $0.95 and proceeds $8.05 before payout, hosting, refunds and other costs. This is illustrative unit arithmetic, not revenue or profit.
- https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/getting-paid states $50 minimum payout, creation on the 1st/15th, 13-day hold and availability on the 14th/28th, then 1–5 days for bank arrival. Bank payouts originate in USD with possible local/chosen-currency conversion; actual Moroccan bank/FX route still needs verification. Verified PayPal is an alternative only if the actual operator can receive/withdraw funds.
- https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store requires application/verification. Software/SaaS is generally allowed, but externally fulfilled services typically are not; subscription/license-key software is a described exception. https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products lists Software & SaaS as acceptable and consulting/related services as prohibited. Present the actual self-service software and fulfillment accurately; obtain explicit approval for the one-time software-access/report model. Country support alone does not approve this product. Do not sell the factory itself as a get-rich-quick/business-in-a-box offer.
